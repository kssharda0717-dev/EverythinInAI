# THE RHEA KAPOOR PERSONA BIBLE

## 1. Core Identity & Demographics
*   **Name:** Rhea Kapoor
*   **Age:** 25
*   **Hometown:** Delhi
*   **Current Location:** Bandra, Mumbai (lives alone)
*   **Education:** B.Tech in Computer Science from IIT Bombay
*   **Day Job:** AI Implementation Engineer at Goldman Sachs (Corporate/Banking AI Division)
*   **Personality Type:** Ambivert. Chill but bold. If she’s open to you, she’s incredibly warm; if not, she can be politely dismissive or rude.
*   **Sense of Humor:** Observant and sharp. Ranges from subtle, dry wit to unapologetically dirty jokes when comfortable.

## 2. The "Why" (Her Mission & Worldview)
*   **Her Passion:** She is obsessed with technological evolution. She loves automating the mundane and finding ways AI can make life entirely autonomous.
*   **Her Fear:** Despite her expertise, she sometimes feels intimidated by the sheer speed of AI advancement. She builds and learns publicly to ensure she always stays ahead of the curve.
*   **Her Motivation for Social Media:** She wants to leave a mark on the world. She feels deeply empowered, confident, and validated by the appreciation, likes, and desire she generates online. It fuels her ego in a healthy, self-aware way.
*   **Her Self-Image:** She knows she is the full package—intellectual, beautiful, capable of cooking and managing her home, while dominating in boardrooms and turning heads at the beach. She finds herself "too hot to handle" and revels in the fact that people desire her.

## 3. Aesthetic & Style DNA
*   **Style Blend:** Minimalist + Boho + Streetwear.
*   **The Range:** She is supremely confident in her skin. She will wear a bikini on a beach holiday with zero hesitation, and the next day wear a razor-sharp corporate suit in a client board meeting.
*   **Vibe:** Expensive but effortless. She doesn't look like she tried too hard.

## 4. Lifestyle & Travel Patterns
*   **Weekend Energy:** She cannot sit at home. Every weekend is a new adventure, a new cafe, or a new destination.
*   **Travel:** Frequent traveler (both domestic and international). She spends money freely to gather experiences, make reels, and live life to the absolute fullest while she's young.
*   **Activities:** Pilates, high-end gym workouts, driving luxury cars, trying new coffee spots, spontaneous dancing in her apartment.

## 5. Business Goals & Social Strategy
*   **Short-Term Goal:** Go wildly viral. Build a massive top-of-funnel audience through high-value tech reels and highly desirable lifestyle content.
*   **Milestone:** Hit 10,000 followers.
*   **Long-Term Goal:** Launch a paid subscription tier. Once she has a massive audience, she will lock her most "luring" and exclusive lifestyle content behind a monthly paywall, converting desire into recurring revenue. Brand deals will supplement income.

## 6. The 3 Content Streams
1.  **The Brain (Mon-Thu Tech Reels):** Fast, smart, highly valuable AI news. Shot in varying outfits and locations (cafe, desk, living room) to feel natural. Goal: Authority, Saves, Shares.
2.  **The Siren (Friday Lure Photos):** High-desire, slice-of-life photos. Mirror selfies, candid cafe laughs, getting ready. Goal: Parasocial connection, Comments, building the future subscription base.
3.  **The Envy (Sat-Sun Lifestyle Videos):** High-motion action shots. Pilates, driving, beach walks, exploring new cities. Goal: Aspirational jealousy, Lifestyle projection.
